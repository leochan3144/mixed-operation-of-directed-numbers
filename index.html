Directed numbers

    Score0

    Work with positive and negative numbers, one line at a time.

    Seven question types from your worksheet: order of operations, fractions, brackets, word problems and the number line. Write each line of working after the equals sign and earn the trophy.

    How it works

    • Each step is a new line starting with =. Type the whole line on the number pad.
    • Correct line: +10. A line with the wrong value: −10 and you get a hint.
    • You can skip steps. A correct line that jumps ahead earns the marks for every step it covers. A correct line of your own is kept too. Both 12 − 10 and (+12) + (−10) are fine.
    • Put negative numbers in brackets: 3 × (−4), not 3 × −4. The ÷ key divides; the fraction key draws a fraction bar.
    • Make a mistake and you’ll answer one more question of the same type. Finish a question with no mistakes to clear the type. Clear all 7 to win the trophy.
    • Sound is on. Use the speaker button at the top to turn it off.
    Your line

    The fraction key puts the next number underneath: (−48), fraction, 6 gives (−48) over 6. For a sum on top or bottom, wrap it in brackets. Keyboard works too: digits, + − * ( ) . , : for ÷, / for a fraction, Enter to check.
